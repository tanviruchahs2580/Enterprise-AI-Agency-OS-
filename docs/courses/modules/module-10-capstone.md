## Certification rubric

Pass requires **all five axes at "meets"**; "exceeds" on ≥3 axes earns distinction.

| Axis | Meets | Exceeds |
|---|---|---|
| Process fidelity | all gate artefacts present and filed | logs enable perfect reproduction by a stranger |
| Visual quality | coherent presentable set | portfolio/marketing grade |
| Concept separation | 5 pairwise-distinct directions | distinct AND narratively defensible |
| Governance | policy followed; disclosure present | proposes an accepted policy/catalog improvement |
| Efficiency | within time + credit targets | ≥30% under target at equal quality |

Assessment protocol: self-assess against rubric → swap projects with a peer for blind reproduction audit (they regenerate one final from your bundle alone) → viz lead signs certificate.
